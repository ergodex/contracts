# Security

Found a problem in how ErgoDEX builds, checks or fills transactions, or a hash here that does not match the chain?

- Email **[hello@ergodex.io](mailto:hello@ergodex.io)**, or write to [t.me/ErgoDexio](https://t.me/ErgoDexio) and ask for a private channel.
- Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.
- The same contacts are in [security.txt](https://ergodex.io/.well-known/security.txt). We answer in English or Russian.

The contracts themselves belong to their authors (see the Source column in the [README](README.md)); report a flaw in a contract to its author as well.
